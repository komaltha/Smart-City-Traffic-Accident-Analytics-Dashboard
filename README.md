# Smart-City-Traffic-Accident-Analytics-Dashboard
🚦 **Project Showcase: Smart City — Traffic & Accident Analytics Dashboard | Python | Streamlit**

I’m excited to share my **Smart City Traffic & Accident Analytics Dashboard**, an interactive data analytics project focused on understanding urban traffic patterns, road accidents, and city safety insights.

This dashboard transforms traffic and accident data into meaningful visualizations to help identify accident-prone zones, analyze traffic conditions, and support data-driven decision-making for smarter and safer cities. 🏙️

📊 **This dashboard includes the following sections:**

🔹 **1. Overview Dashboard**  
Provides a quick summary of important metrics, including total accidents, total injuries, total fatalities, and the most affected city zone.

🔹 **2. Accident Trend Analysis**  
Analyzes accident trends over time and compares accident counts across different city zones and weather conditions.

🔹 **3. Road Condition Analysis**  
Visualizes accidents based on road conditions such as dry, wet, slippery, and other conditions to identify potential risk factors.

🔹 **4. Accident Hotspot Analysis**  
Highlights accident-prone areas across the city to help identify locations that may require improved road safety measures.

🔹 **5. Traffic Flow Analysis**  
Provides insights into vehicle movement, including cars, two-wheelers, public transport, and heavy vehicles.

🔹 **6. Recent Accident Records**  
Displays accident details, locations, accident types, severity levels, and investigation status.
